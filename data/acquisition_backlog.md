The [HCPA DOR Code Manual audit](../runs/u0-hcpa-dor-manual-20260928T195353Z/report.md) documents local `0100` as detached single-family residential, `0400` as condominium and `0800` as small multifamily. The current 2026-09-25 parcel ZIP's `parcel_dor_names.dbf` independently confirms these three broad categories; see [the current parcel source card](source_cards/hillsborough_hcpa_current_parcels.yaml). Neither source proves historical code availability, a dwelling-level transaction or sale eligibility. The [strict parcel-link audit](../runs/u0-hcpa-parcel-links-20260928T203944Z/report.md) found zero exact PIN+FOLIO matches on the frozen 200-row sample and 200 FOLIO-only leads; none was accepted as a join. A [disjoint 1,000-row crosswalk audit](../runs/u0-hcpa-pin-crosswalk-20260928T214110Z/report.md) subsequently found agreement for 983/983 distinct 2025 STRAP controls and 994/994 nonblank current PINs under the registered conversion. Six current PINs were blank and 16 sample rows lacked a 2025 exact control. Automatic joining remains blocked by unconfirmed source semantics, rights, historical availability and sale identity. Row-level flags remain outside Git.

The [2025 parcel archive inventory](../runs/u0-hcpa-2025-vintage-20260928T203518Z/report.md) verifies a separately pinned annual ZIP and its field schema. It has a formatted `PIN` C(29), while the inspected current 2026 parcel DBF has `PIN` C(25). The schema difference requires a vintage-specific identity check. Neither the 2025 archive's name nor the listing's update date proves when its attributes first became available to a historical valuation.

The [frozen crosswalk-gap diagnostic](../runs/u0-hcpa-pin-gaps-20260928T222200Z/report.md) found six unique 2025 FOLIO-only leads whose raw PINs were blank and STRAPs agreed with the candidate transform, plus ten rows with no 2025 one-key lead. Six current PIN fields were raw blank; five overlap those old leads. These are private-review priorities, not accepted joins or a source availability rule.

The [NYC bounded API inventory](../runs/u0-nyc-api-inventory-20260928T220444Z/report.md) captured and hashed only aggregate responses: 82,345 current rolling-view rows and 845,607 current annualized-view rows. The latter is not a count of all linked DOF annual files. No row-level data, unique-transfer count or certified historical availability was established; rank and admission status are unchanged.

[ADR 0019](../decisions/0019-nyc-rolling-archive-correction.md) corrects the earlier statement that NYC Open Data retains no older rolling-view versions. The [bounded metadata probe](../runs/u0-nyc-archive-metadata-20260929T021258Z/report.md) verified 13 visible revision records (versions 53-65) from the portal's internal read-only route. Later bounded GET-only capture recovered versions 61 and 62; neither was admitted for historical use. Revision creation time does not prove any row's first availability. The earlier metadata probe excluded the CSV endpoint because its status and generation behavior had not yet been verified.

The [NYC Open Data use review](source_cards/nyc_open_data_use_review.yaml) records the City's general reuse guidance and the portal's additional-term caveat. It supports bounded internal source qualification, while dataset-specific commercial release and row redistribution decisions remain pending. ACRIS website bandwidth guidance is tracked separately from the Open Data API used by the pilots.

The [frozen NYC rolling snapshot](../runs/u0-nyc-rolling-snapshot-20260928T225512Z/report.md) and [200-row private sample](../runs/u0-nyc-review-sample-20260928T234700Z/report.md) now replace the older API-only evidence for that *current* view; ten review-effort rubrics are complete, but zero sale labels are certified. The [ACRIS metadata inventory](../runs/u0-nyc-acris-inventory-20260929T000000Z/report.md) identifies official Master/Legals linkage candidates in boroughs 1–4, before the sampled lookup. The [frozen pilot](../runs/u0-nyc-acris-pilot-20260929T003102Z/report.md) then stopped at a linked-Legals cap after three requests; zero rows were matched. Its protocol remains in [ADR 0022](../decisions/0022-nyc-acris-linkage-pilot.md). The [official 126-code inventory](../runs/u0-nyc-acris-codes-20260929T004355Z/report.md) maps `CDEC` to a condo declaration; the broad conveyances class is insufficient for sale matching. The ACRIS amount and dates cannot independently certify a sale price or 90-day as-of origin. The [v2 ACRIS route](../runs/u0-nyc-acris-v2-20260929T012700Z/report.md) reused the same four selected records and preserved every bounded response. It remains incomplete after valid saturation and duplicate Master IDs; private state replays without new requests. No sale price, close date or historical availability was certified, and source-specific release rights remain undecided.

These candidates span all four Census regions on paper, but no market has passed the source audit or G-US. Florida and Maryland contain possible nonmetro counties; two strata still must be selected and tested. Select the eventual eight metros and two strata before tuning. A usable source must supply verifiable target semantics, dated features and an availability rule; a high raw row count does not compensate for those gaps.

The [bounded NYC version-61 capture](../runs/u0-nyc-ready-archive-v61-20261002T230327Z/report.md) has 79,335 rows and is privately hashed and offline replayed. The independently captured version 62 has 81,567 rows. The [frozen adjacent-archive comparison](../runs/u0-nyc-adjacent-archives-v1-20261002T233435Z/report.md) found at least 1,000 matching 21-field source representations both before and after normalizing only the sale-date representation. Exact overlap and residual counts remain private. A row-count difference or representation overlap cannot establish economic-transfer identity, row additions/deletions, corrections or first publication. Both archives remain inventory-only because first row availability, sale/close-date meaning, transaction and unit identity, source-specific release rights and historical feature vintages remain unresolved. NYC certified sale labels remain zero; its backlog rank and admission status are unchanged.

When a source qualifies, register its exact file and checksum, preserve it unchanged, audit at least 200 stratified records, and measure join multiplicity and missingness. Rank any later paid acquisition by expected reduction in consequential error per price and implementation effort, with an explicit monetary authorisation before purchase.

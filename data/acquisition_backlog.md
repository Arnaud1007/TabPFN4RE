These candidates cover only the Northeast, Midwest and West regions and do not establish even one accepted G-US market. Find additional official or licensed sources in the South and two nonmetropolitan strata. Select the eventual eight metros and two strata before tuning. A usable source must supply verifiable target semantics, dated features and an availability rule; a high raw row count does not compensate for those gaps.

When a source qualifies, register its exact file and checksum, preserve it unchanged, audit at least 200 stratified records, and measure join multiplicity and missingness. Rank any later paid acquisition by expected reduction in consequential error per price and implementation effort, with an explicit monetary authorisation before purchase.
